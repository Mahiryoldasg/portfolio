## Problem

Merchants wanted to offer Kredim credit to their own customers, but had no
surface of their own to do it from. Anything a merchant needed went through
Kredim's internal teams, which put a person in the loop of a process that should
have belonged to the merchant.

## Architecture

A partner-facing panel on the same platform as the Member Panel - React 18,
Redux Toolkit slices, Redux-Saga for async flows - rather than a new stack for
a new audience. The credit, campaign, and authentication logic already lived
there; what Merchant added was a different tenant with different permissions
sitting on top of it.
