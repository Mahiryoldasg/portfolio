## Problem

Paying with Kredim credit meant leaving whatever app you were already in. A
customer partway through checkout on a partner's e-commerce site, or inside a
banking app, had to break context to complete a payment, and every context
break is somewhere a transaction can be abandoned.

## Architecture

An embeddable checkout flow that runs inside third-party e-commerce and banking
partner applications, rather than a destination the customer is sent to. The
flow carries campaign and installment selection with it, so the choices that
used to require a separate step happen in place.

It runs on the same platform as the Member Panel - React 18 with Redux Toolkit
slices and Redux-Saga for the async sequences - so the payment and credit logic
already proven there did not have to be rebuilt for an embedded context.

## Decisions

Interrupted transactions get an SMS-triggered deep link back into the flow,
rather than being dropped. An embedded surface has more ways to be interrupted
than a page you own end to end - the host app backgrounds, a call comes in -
so recovery had to be a designed path, not an error state.
